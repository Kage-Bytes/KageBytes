KageBytes is an underground-style IT collective built for people who live and breathe tech..

An independent open-source organization dedicated to cybersecurity, security engineering, software development, and technical research. We investigate complex systems, develop security tools, and explore the underlying technologies that shape the digital world. Our focus goes beyond the surface, seeking to understand architectures, uncover weaknesses, and examine how systems can be made more resilient.

We value technical depth over appearances, independent thinking over recognition, and meaningful contributions over credentials. Through research, experimentation, and responsible development, we transform complex ideas into practical tools and accessible knowledge.

We believe you can't protect what you don't understand, so we learn by building, breaking, and rebuilding, and we use that knowledge to strengthen systems, never to harm them. KageBytes is currently invite-only.
