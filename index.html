KageBytes is a small IT collective focused on cybersecurity.

We are a group of builders, tinkerers and breakers who learn by doing. We study how systems fail so we can help make them stronger, and we keep things quiet, practical and honest.

What we do

  • Security research — learning how things break, responsibly.
  • Building tools — small, useful projects shared with the community.
  • Learning together — sharing notes, writeups and ideas.

Our values

  • Minimal — no noise, only what matters.
  • Curious — always asking "what if?"
  • Ethical — we protect, we don't harm.
